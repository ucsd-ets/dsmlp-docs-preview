For students, instructors, and researchers

Computing for Every Course That Needs It

DSMLP gives UC San Diego students and instructors Jupyter, RStudio, and GPUs for scheduled classes, independent study, and student projects. Researchers use the same cluster for work outside a course.

Datahub is the browser interface to DSMLP. You can also work from the command line.

New for Fall 2026

GPU Reservations

Every GPU session runs under a reservation. Book a window ahead of time in the reservation app, or launch on demand and the reservation system creates a lease for you. Both draw on your workspace's Service Unit budget, so there is no free exploratory launch.

New GPU servers arrived in Fall 2026 to expand GPU capacity for instruction.
