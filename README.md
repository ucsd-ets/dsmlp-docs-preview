# dsmlp-docs-preview

**This is the preview site for the DSMLP documentation. It is not the
published documentation, and it is not where the source lives.**

Read the real thing at <https://dsmlp-docs.ucsd.edu/> — source at
[`ucsd-ets/dsmlp-docs-public`](https://github.com/ucsd-ets/dsmlp-docs-public).

Pages here are staged for review before publication. They may be
unfinished, unreviewed or wrong, and they can change or disappear
without notice. Nothing here should be linked to, cited, or relied on.

The contents of this repository are generated. Commits are not made
here by hand, and anything committed directly is overwritten by the
next promotion. To report a problem with the *published*
documentation, [open an issue on the public
repository](https://github.com/ucsd-ets/dsmlp-docs-public/issues/new/choose).
