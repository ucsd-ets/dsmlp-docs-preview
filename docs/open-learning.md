# Open Learning

Open Learning gives students in eligible majors and programs a workspace on
Datahub and DSMLP from Winter 2027, for coursework and exploration outside a
course workspace or a sponsored project. You are added from your major or
program, with nothing to request. [Eligible Programs](#eligible-programs) lists
the programs.

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| Who | Students in the programs in [Eligible Programs](#eligible-programs) |
| Opens | Winter 2027 |
| Access | Nothing to request. You are added from your major or program in TSS (formerly TritonLink) |
| Peak weeks | Lower GPU limits, with coursework served first |

## Eligible Programs

From Winter 2027, students in these programs are members of the Open Learning
workspace that Datahub lists as
**Open Learning - Halıcıoğlu School Affiliates**.

| Program | Level |
|---|---|
| Bachelor of Science in Data Science | Undergraduate major |
| Minor in Data Science | Undergraduate minor |
| Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence | Undergraduate major |
| Master of Science in Data Science (MSDS) | Graduate |
| Master of Data Science Online (MDS) | Graduate |
| Master of Advanced Studies in Data Science and Engineering (MAS-DSE) | Graduate |
| Ph.D. in Data Science | Graduate |

Other schools can offer Open Learning to their students. If your program is not
listed and you would use Open Learning, tell your dean's office.

## Membership

You are added to Open Learning from your major, minor or graduate program as
recorded in TSS (formerly TritonLink). A second major or a minor in an eligible
program counts. [Open Learning](access/when-access-starts-and-ends.md#open-learning)
says how soon a change in TSS reaches the workspace, and how long access lasts
after graduation.

> [!WARNING]
> When your access ends, the workspace disappears from your account, and so
> does your Open Learning home directory. Copy out anything you want to keep
> before you leave an eligible program, or before your account is removed
> after graduation. See
> [Retrieving Work Before Access Ends](workspaces-and-storage/moving-and-sharing-data.md#retrieving-work-before-access-ends).

## What Open Learning Is For

| Work | Where it belongs |
|---|---|
| Coursework in a course that has its own Datahub workspace | The course workspace. See [Using Datahub in a Course](student-in-a-course.md) |
| Coursework in a course without its own workspace | Open Learning |
| Practice, tutorials, and trying out tools outside any course | Open Learning |
| Independent study, a capstone, or a club, team or personal project with a faculty sponsor | A project workspace. See [Projects & Independent Study](student-project.md) |
| Research | [Research on DSMLP](individual-researcher.md) |

## Priority in Peak Weeks

In peak weeks, weeks 6 to 10 of a quarter and finals week, coursework is served
ahead of Open Learning work, and the Open Learning workspace has lower GPU
limits than at other times. The limits are lower, not zero. See
[Peak GPU Demand](reference/policy.md#peak-gpu-demand).

Open Learning does not replace a course's own GPU access. If a course you take
will need substantial GPU time in peak weeks, ask the instructor to request GPU
access for the course. See
[Requesting a Course](instructor-or-ta.md#requesting-a-course).

## Working in Open Learning

Open Learning is a workspace, so you reach it the same ways as a course
workspace. In Datahub at [datahub.ucsd.edu](https://datahub.ucsd.edu), select
**Open Learning - Halıcıoğlu School Affiliates**. From the login node, name the
workspace with `-W OLE_HSDSC`.

| Task | Documented in |
|---|---|
| Open Jupyter or RStudio in a browser | [Datahub in the Browser](access/datahub-in-the-browser.md) |
| Choose the Open Learning workspace at launch | [Belonging to Several Workspaces](workspaces-and-storage/what-a-workspace-is.md#belonging-to-several-workspaces) |
| Connect from a terminal | [Connecting over SSH](access/the-login-node.md#connecting-over-ssh) |
| Name the workspace in a launch command | [Naming a Workspace on the Command Line](workspaces-and-storage/what-a-workspace-is.md#naming-a-workspace-on-the-command-line) |
| Install packages | [Customizing an Environment](environments/customizing-your-environment.md) |
| Book or launch a GPU session | [Reservations](gpu-access/reservations.md) |
| Find out why a session stopped | [What Ends a Session](gpu-access/what-ends-a-session.md) |

### Files

Your Open Learning home directory is separate from your home directory in each
course. A file you save in one is not visible from the other. Keep a file you
need in both in `private/`, which appears in every workspace you belong to. See
[Per-Workspace Home Directories](workspaces-and-storage/what-a-workspace-is.md#per-workspace-home-directories).

### GPU Classes and Service Units

The Open Learning workspace has access to every GPU class. See
[GPU Classes](gpu-access/gpu-classes.md). Its GPU quotas and Service Unit
budget are not yet published.

> [!WARNING]
> Every GPU session in Open Learning draws on your Service Unit budget in the
> workspace, whether you booked it or launched on demand. See
> [On-Demand Lease Charges](gpu-access/service-units-and-budgets.md#on-demand-lease-charges).

[Remaining Balance](gpu-access/service-units-and-budgets.md#remaining-balance)
shows how to check what is left.

## Support

Open Learning has no instructor or TA. Questions about Open Learning or the
platform go to [datahub@ucsd.edu](mailto:datahub@ucsd.edu) or the
[ITS Service Desk](https://support.ucsd.edu/). A problem report from Open
Learning may take longer to answer than one from a course. See
[Individual User Issues](reference/getting-help.md#individual-user-issues). A
question about a course's assignments still goes to that course's instructor or
TA.
